1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This document specifies the software requirements for PowerHour Release 1. It defines the system behaviour, interfaces, data, business rules and non-functional requirements needed for implementation and verification by developers, QA and UI/UX designers.
1.2 Product scope
PowerHour is a time-slot based tutoring platform connecting students with verified teachers for one-to-one, one-hour online lessons. Students select the lesson requirements and an available teacher, complete payment through the platform, attend the lesson in the lesson room, and may rate the teacher afterwards. Teachers publish their expertise and availability and receive payment for completed lessons. The platform commission is 10% of the lesson fee.
In scope (Release 1):
Student and teacher registration, verification and account management
Teacher availability management and hour-slot booking
Subject, topic and time based teacher matching, with ratings and dynamic hourly rates
Teacher confirmation workflow, reminders and push notifications
In-app online lesson room: chat, voice, document sharing, digital whiteboard and screen sharing
In-app e-wallet, top-up via card and local wallets, and automatic commission deduction
Post-lesson teacher rating
Student, teacher and administrator dashboards and analytics
Out of scope (Release 1):
Group or collective lessons and cost-splitting packages
Physical “PowerHour Lecture Rooms”
Non-academic consultation verticals such as legal or expert advice
Pre-recorded content or a learning management system (LMS)
International expansion

1.5 Intended use
Students register, select lesson requirements and an available teacher, submit a booking request, attend the confirmed lesson in the lesson room, and complete payment through the platform. Teachers manage availability, respond to booking requests and conduct confirmed lessons.

2. System Overview
2.1 Product perspective
PowerHour is a client–server system delivered as Android, iOS and web clients sharing one backend. It uses external services for payment processing, push notifications, OTP delivery and real-time communication.
Component
Responsibility
Client apps (Android, iOS, web)
Presentation, input validation, lesson room client, local notifications.
Application backend / API
Business logic: accounts, availability, booking lifecycle, matching, ratings, wallet ledger, notifications.
Database
Persistent storage of users, bookings, messages, ratings and financial transactions.
Real-time communication service
Chat, voice, video, screen sharing and whiteboard synchronisation.
Third-party integrations
Payment gateway(s), push/SMS/email providers, analytics.
Admin console
Operations, verification, configuration and reporting (web only).
2.2 Product workflow
Students and teachers register and verify their accounts. Teachers declare expertise and working hours and fund their wallet.
A student starts a booking, selecting language, subject, topic, level and preferred hour.
The app lists teachers who match the subject, are available in that hour and are not already booked. Each entry shows name, photo, average rating, lectures taught and hourly rate.
The student selects a teacher; the app sends a confirmation request to the teacher including subject, topic and time.
On acceptance, the student is notified and the booking appears in “My Bookings” for both parties; a chat channel opens.
Both parties receive reminders as the lesson approaches.
At the scheduled time the lesson takes place in the in-app lesson room.
On completion, the lesson fee is settled, the 10% commission is deducted from the teacher’s wallet, and the student rates the teacher.
The rating updates the teacher’s average rating and future hourly rate.
2.3 User classes and characteristics
User class
Description
Key needs
Student
School, college or university learner (may be a minor). Occasional, topic-specific help; price sensitive; typically mobile-first.
Fast search, transparent price, trusted teachers, simple payment.
Teacher
Qualified individual offering tuition in one or more subjects during self-defined hours.
Predictable schedule, fair rate growth, clear earnings view, reliable payment.
Administrator
PowerHour staff with privileged access via the admin console.
Verification tools, analytics, dispute handling, configuration control.
2.4 Operating environment
Android and iOS (current and previous two major versions) and modern evergreen web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
Internet connectivity is mandatory; Release 1 has no offline lesson capability.
Backend hosted on a cloud platform in a region with acceptable latency to Pakistan.
2.5 Design and implementation constraints
Payments and regulation: holding user balances in an e-wallet may be regulated (for example by the State Bank of Pakistan). The wallet should be implemented through a licensed payment partner unless PowerHour obtains the required licence. See Open Issue OI-05.
Minors: primary, middle and matric students are likely to be under 18; the design shall accommodate parental consent and enhanced safeguarding (NFR-PRV-03).

6. Business Rules
ID
Rule
BR-01
Each lesson lasts one hour and starts on the hour.
BR-02
The platform commission is 10% of the lesson fee, deducted from the teacher’s wallet after each completed lesson. The percentage is configurable by administrators.
BR-03
A teacher shall hold at least the minimum wallet balance (amount TBD, OI-03) to be listed for bookings; the minimum ensures commission can always be collected.
BR-04
Ratings are integers from 1 to 5. Average rating is the arithmetic mean of all ratings received.
BR-05
Hourly rate depends on average rating and total lectures taught; the rate at the time of booking is the rate charged.
BR-06
Cancellation policy (proposed): free cancellation up to 6 hours before start; later student cancellation forfeits a percentage of the fee to the teacher; teacher cancellation always refunds the student in full and may reduce the teacher’s standing.
BR-07
No-shows: if the teacher fails to attend, the student is fully refunded; if the student fails to attend, the teacher is paid the fee less commission. Disputes are resolved by an administrator.
BR-08
A time slot can be held by only one booking at a time for a given teacher, and a student cannot hold overlapping slots.
BR-09
Only students who completed a lesson with a teacher may rate that teacher.

7.3 Data retention
Financial and audit records shall be retained for the period required by law (proposed: at least 6 years).
Chat content and shared files shall be retained for a defined period (proposed: 12 months) to support disputes, then deleted or anonymised.
8. Requirements Requiring Confirmation

ID
Issue
Proposed resolution
OI-01
The formula linking average rating and lectures taught to hourly rate is not defined.
Define base rate per level plus tiered multipliers (for example rating bands and lecture-count bands); make it configurable.
OI-02
